Privacy Policy

1. Overview

MotionHype AI provides AI-generated text responses using a language model downloaded to, and run on, your Android device. The core text chat feature is designed to work without an internet connection after the model has been downloaded.

Core chat does not require an RBAPPS account or sign-in.

Offline by design: For ordinary offline chat, AI prompts and responses are processed locally on the device, rather than being sent to an RBAPPS-hosted AI inference server. Separate internet-enabled features, advertising, or communications may involve third-party services, as explained below.

2. Information handled

Depending on the features you use, MotionHype AI may handle:

  • Chat content: text messages, AI responses, conversation names, and related timestamps stored on your device.
  • Local AI files: downloaded language-model files and necessary local configuration.
  • App preferences: settings such as chat preferences, display options, and available privacy choices.
  • Technical and advertising information: advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), device and app information, IP-based approximate location, interactions with ads, diagnostic signals, fraud-prevention signals, and consent status, which may be handled by Google's advertising services.
  • Support information: information you choose to include if you contact RBAPPS or submit feedback or an AI-response report.

RBAPPS does not require your name, phone number, or government identification to use ordinary offline chat.

3. Offline AI processing and local storage

Once the AI model is available locally, normal text prompts are processed on your device. Chat messages and conversation history are saved in app-private storage to support reopening existing conversations. Model weights are also stored in app-private storage.

RBAPPS does not operate a remote inference server for the offline text-chat feature. Local processing does not mean the entire app has no network activity: model downloads, advertising and optional web search require internet access.

AI output: AI-generated text can be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. Do not rely on it as a substitute for professional advice in high-stakes situations.

4. Optional internet search

If a Web Search or similar online information feature is available in your installed app version and you choose to use it, the search query and necessary request information may be sent over the internet to the configured search service or backend. Search providers may receive technical network information such as your IP address. Search results may then be supplied as context to the on-device AI model to generate an answer.

The privacy terms of the actual search provider or backend will apply. RBAPPS will update this policy with specific providers or additional processing details before such a feature is made available in a production release.

If web search is not offered or is disabled, normal offline chat does not use a web search provider. Do not include sensitive personal information in online searches.

5. Permissions and device access

  • Internet: used for AI model downloads, advertising, consent messages, and any optional online search capability.
  • Network state: used to check connection availability for network-dependent features.
  • Storage: the core text-chat feature uses Android app-private storage and does not require broad storage access.
  • Camera, microphone, and photos: not required for the current text-only core chat feature.

Some Google SDKs may contribute additional permissions or components to the final Android package, such as those related to advertising identifiers or measurement. The permissions shown in Android settings depend on the installed app version and bundled SDKs.

6. Advertising with Google AdMob

MotionHype AI integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising. Google and advertising partners may process certain information to request, deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. This may include advertising identifiers where available, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app data, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or security data.

Depending on applicable law, consent, device settings and availability, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. Ads require an internet connection. No ad being visible at a particular moment does not necessarily mean the SDK is inactive.

For details, see Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7. Consent and privacy choices

MotionHype AI integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present advertising-related consent or privacy messages where applicable. Availability and content of those forms depend on region, Google settings, and applicable rules.

Where required, a privacy choices control may be available inside the app to review or change eligible advertising choices. System-level options, such as Android advertising privacy controls, may also affect data processing.

8. Third-party services and data sharing

Services that may be involved in the installed version include:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK: ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Google Play services and Android platform services: relevant device, app, and advertising support.
  • Model download hosting: the model download host may receive standard network request information during a download.
  • Optional web search service: only if an internet search feature is offered and used.

RBAPPS does not intentionally send the text of offline conversations to advertising providers for AI response generation. Third-party SDKs may independently collect or process the technical information described here according to their policies.

We do not sell users' locally stored chat contents. Information you intentionally send through support, reporting, links, or external sharing may be handled by the selected service or recipient.

9. Feedback, support, and AI-content reports

When you choose to contact RBAPPS, your message and any information you include are transmitted through the communication method you use. If your app version offers a Report or Flag action, the exact delivery method may vary by release. For example, when it opens an email application, the report is sent only if you complete the email-send action.

For your privacy, avoid including passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal details in reports. Support messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve an issue, maintain safety, or meet legal obligations.

Privacy questions and feedback can be sent to digitalcreatorranjeet@gmail.com.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Chat history: stays in app-private storage until you delete individual conversations, use Delete All Chats (where available), clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Local AI model: stays in app-private storage until removed using available app/Android storage controls or when app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled. Deleting chat history is not intended to remove model files.
  • Settings: remain until changed or removed through app/Android controls; applicable Android backup settings may affect restoration.
  • Advertising and consent information: retention follows applicable Google policies, settings and legal requirements.
  • Support messages: retained as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes.

If a version provides no RBAPPS account, there is no separate RBAPPS-hosted chat account to delete. Deleting local conversations does not automatically delete data that a third-party advertising or search provider may have collected.

11. Security

MotionHype AI uses Android app-private storage for local chats and model data and uses secure network transport for supported online connections. We aim to limit unnecessary access to personal information. However, no app, device, communication service, or internet connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Protect your device with appropriate security settings and avoid placing sensitive data into public web searches or support messages.

12. Children's privacy

MotionHype AI is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The app does not ask users to create an account to access ordinary offline chat. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to RBAPPS, please contact us so we can review the concern.

Any age restrictions or audience selections in the Play Store listing should reflect the app's final configuration and advertising settings.

13. International processing

Google advertising, consent, download, and optional search providers may process technical information on systems located in countries other than your own, according to their policies and applicable legal protections. Ordinary offline AI chat runs on your device as described above.

14. Changes to this policy

RBAPPS may update this policy to reflect app features, data practices, SDK changes, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.
